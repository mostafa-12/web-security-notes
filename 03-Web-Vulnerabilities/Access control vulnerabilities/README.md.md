
# What is Access control 

Access control is the application of constraints on who or what is authorized to perform actions or access resources

---

# Types of Auth 

There is Two types of Auth **Authentications and Authorization**

---

## Authentication 

**Who are u**, there is many types of methods to identify identity of user and based in this knowledge u can identify his privileges and other important info about user

---
### Types of Authentication

1. something u know (Username and Password) 
2.  something u have (tokens by software or hardware, ur phone )
3.  something u are (fingerprint or face_id )

---
### Methods
1. password-based: username and password
2. OTP (one time password): sending to email or phone
3. biometric : fingerprint , face id
4. MFA (Multi factor authentications) : collection of last methods 
5. OAuth: login by another trusted platform like google Account or github and like that
6. Token-Based : JWT, Cookies 

---


## Authorization

what u can do (privileges ), what u are allowed to do and what u are not

---
### Methods 

1. Role-Based: privileges based on Roles (like : admin, manager, normal user, gust)
2. Object-Based
3. Attribute-Based: user attribute (age, location, ....etc)
4. Time-Based: working time
