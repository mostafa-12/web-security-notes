## Cookies
- Cookies are stored as **name=value** pairs.
- The server sends cookies using **Set-Cookie**.
- The browser automatically returns them in the **Cookie** header on future requests.
- Multiple cookies are separated by `;`
### Set Cookies by server in response 
~~~
Set-Cookie: tracking=tI8rk7joMx44S2Uu85nSWc
~~~
  
### Auto Attach Cookies by browser in request 
 
~~~
Cookie: tracking=tI8rk7joMx44S2Uu85nSWc
~~~
